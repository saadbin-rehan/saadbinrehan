Questions people ask

How many panels will I need?

It depends on your monthly units. Use the calculator at the top for a starting figure, then we confirm it during a site inspection.

What's the difference between on-grid and hybrid?

An on-grid system works together with the grid and lowers your bill. A hybrid system adds batteries so you also have backup during outages. We'll recommend one after checking your usage.

Do dust and haze affect output?

Yes. Dust and smog reduce production a little, so panels should be cleaned regularly. We'll explain how to look after your system.
